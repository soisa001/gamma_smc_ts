# Surviving tags for completed sweeps

Seven previously incomplete strong-selection treatments retain every completed segregating replicate. Open slots are filled from archived histories in a predetermined order, with distinct founding genealogies within each treatment. The two previously omitted ongoing 50-kya treatments at s = 0.01 already contain 100 segregating replicates each.

For each fixed history we replay the original SLiM seed through the present and verify that every focal copy count and population size through fixation equals the archived trajectory. At the last generation with focal frequency at or below 80% before fixation, we remember up to 1,000 diploids sampled with a separate seed. We add neutral mutations jointly on historical and modern genealogies. Within the simulated megabase we rank biallelic historical markers by haplotype r² with the causal allele. Ties are broken by distance from the causal site and then coordinate. We traverse the ranking until the positively associated tag allele segregates among the 100 sampled present-day diploids.

We decode the chosen tag coordinate for each fixed history using the same 1,000 pairs as in the original analysis. A detection requires a pointwise empirical p-value below the stated threshold for at least one 1-kya cutoff inside the selection window. The reference and held-out neutral alleles segregate today. Their empirical FPR describes that original ascertainment. A neutral fixation followed by historical tag selection has a different ascertainment and would require its own calibration.

`rates.tsv` separates the calls among retained original alleles and added tags. The combined rates reflect the requested gap-filling design. Estimating unconditional sweep-detection power would additionally require weighting by the frequencies of fixation, loss, and continued segregation among unconditioned proposals. `inputs.json` records source identities, fixation and snapshot times, tag coordinates, ranks, LD, allele frequencies and checksums. The historical ranking is available as simulation truth.
