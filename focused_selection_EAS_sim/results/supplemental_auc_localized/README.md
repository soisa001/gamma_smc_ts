# Focused selection supplemental AUC and focal-window plots

These figures are additive views of the frozen published analysis. No simulation, decoding, aggregation, statistical testing, or canonical analysis output was rerun.

- AUC figures show the prespecified focal alt/alt-minus-ref/ref score for selected versus matched-neutral replicates across all seven TMRCA thresholds. Bands are the already-published pointwise bootstrap 95% intervals. AUC below 0.5 is retained as a direction reversal.
- Localized figures show +/-100 kb around the focal site on the existing 10-kb grid at 10 kya. Bands are mean +/- 1.96 SEM and do not represent a covariance-aware contrast interval.
- Selected cells contain 10 replicates and matched neutral cells contain 100 replicates. Thresholds and selection-coefficient comparisons reuse correlated inputs.
